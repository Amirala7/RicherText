Design system · Spell check · ADR 0036 · approved 2026-10-29

Spell check: the underline and the suggestions menu

Two pieces for the paid-plan spell checker. Both use only existing tokens, and the menu is the existing menu molecule. Click an underlined word to try it.

What's new

  • Misspelling underline: a wavy line in danger, 1.5px, 3px below the text. It's a decoration, so it's never saved or copied, and it never changes the line height.
  • Suggestions menu: the menu molecule with up to 5 suggestions (500 weight), a divider, then Ignore. With no suggestions it shows "No suggestions" as a menu label.
  • No on/off switch in v1: paid plans always check, and Ignore covers one-off words.

Decided in review (user, 2026-10-29)

  1. Wavy red underline (yes; dotted dropped)
  2. Up to 5 suggestions (changed from 3)
  3. No on/off switch in v1: always on for paid plans (yes)
  4. Faint red tint on the word while its menu is open (yes)
